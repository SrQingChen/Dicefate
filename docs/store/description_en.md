# Dicefate

> **Every blow lands as a dice contest. Out-roll fate itself.**

Your sword swing, the zombie's lunge, the boss's molten slam — with Dicefate installed, **every single hit becomes a dice contest**. Both sides roll their pools in the same tick, totals collide, and the verdict flips over in a 0.1-second settle.

Combat stops being stat-check arithmetic and becomes a game of **reading the board, betting momentum, and building a dice pool that bends fate**.

## ✨ Why you'll like it

- **🎲 Dice Contests** — all player-sourced damage (melee, sweeps, arrows, throws, explosions, minions) runs a full roll → compare → resolve pipeline; mob ranged attacks get light contests, and one swing sweeping a crowd shares a single attack roll
- **⚔️ Poise & Fatebreak** — drain an enemy's poise into *Fate Exhaustion* where every die shatters and rolls 0, then chain guaranteed fate strikes and vulnerability windows
- **📈 Momentum Cash-in** — build momentum through the fight, press `V` to detonate it: all your dice at max vs. all theirs at zero, in a six-second window of glory
- **🧩 Pool Deckbuilding** — start with one d6 slot and grow to six; poker-hand bonuses (straights, triplets, destiny upgrades), 4 elemental reactions, 10 synergies, 8 skill-die families × 3 tiers
- **🧊 Real Polyhedral Dice** — all 83 dice are exact 3D meshes (d4 / d6 / d8 / d10 / d12 / d20 / d24) with numbered faces, rendered as true solids in your hotbar
- **🏛 Handmade Content** — 4 mechanic bosses (the Two-Faced Gambler, the Molten Die King, the Froststill Queen, the Zero-Faced One), 6 fate structures to raid, 33 advancements, a dice-merchant economy with server-locked mystery boxes

## 🎮 30-second start

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `B` | Fate board — slot dice, switch stances, save 3 presets |
| `V` | Cash in momentum when it's (almost) full |
| `G` | Toggle the dice spirit (auto-fixes your lowest die +1) |

**The loop**: craft a die at the Carving Table → slot it with `B` → fight, contest, build momentum → detonate with `V` → reroll inscriptions, unlock slots, chase rarer dice.

## 🔗 Compatibility

Requires **NeoForge 26.1.2+** (Minecraft 26.1.2), Java 25. Optional integrations — Curios (dedicated charm slots), Jade (poise panel, landing in 0.4), JECharacters (pinyin search) — all degrade gracefully when absent.

⚠️ Creative / spectator mode skips contests **by design** — hop into survival for the full system.

Now in **0.3.2 beta**: the core contest pipeline is verified in live survival play; all 83 dice, 4 bosses, 6 structures and 33 advancements are playable. Found a bug or got a spicy idea? Drop it on GitHub Issues — may the dice favor you.
